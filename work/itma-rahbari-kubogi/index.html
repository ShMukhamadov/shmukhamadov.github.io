ITMA Rahbari Kubogi started as a childhood dream of school-to-school football tournaments. With the Agency for Specialized Educational Institutions, I turned it into a national sports and networking cup: 224 schools and more than 7,000 students in its first season.

The story

My family loves football, and as a kid I dreamed about street-against-street and school-against-school tournaments. At school, students kept asking me to connect them with students from other schools, and students elsewhere wanted to meet Presidential School students. So I thought: why not connect both?

In February 2025, I built a team from across the country. In September, our principal, Behruz and I presented the idea to the head of the Agency, and she agreed to support it fully. The opening ceremony was at my school on 26 October 2025. At first, the Agency wanted to include only big cities, because schools from small towns meant higher transport costs. I insisted that every school should play, wherever it was. In the final, a school from a small city won.

My own team lost at the regional stage. My rules applied to us too. I still went to the final, without my team, to organize it for the teams that beat us.

The idea

A childhood dream

Since early childhood, I have loved football, because my family loves it. I dreamed about street-to-street, class-to-class and school-to-school tournaments. Many students at my school also came to me for help: they knew I had a large network outside the school, and they wanted to meet other students too. Students from other schools said they wanted to meet Presidential School students. So I thought: why not connect both? That is how the idea for ITMA Rahbari Kubogi came to me.

In February 2025, I started building a team of students from every corner of the country to help me launch it. In September, my school principal, my co-founder Behruz Chilmuhammedov and I went to the Agency’s office to present the idea to the head of the Agency. She liked it and agreed that the Agency would support it, and we held the opening ceremony at my school, the Presidential School in Jizzakh, where the project was born.

2025 – 2026 season

224 schools, one cup

The cup has 3 stages: group, regional and the republican final. The Agency helped a lot to run it across the country. ITMA Rahbari Kubogi included four sports (mini football, volleyball, table tennis and chess), and in a single academic year it reached 224 schools and 7,000+ students, promoted a healthy lifestyle and gave students a way to build a network. We made sure the tournament was fair. Even though it was my project, my own school’s team lost at the regional stage and did not reach the final. I went to the final only as its organizer. The main purpose was to build a bridge between schools across the country, so students could build a network and use it in their future careers.

Every year

A cup that travels

Each year, the final is held at the school that won the cup the year before, so the cup travels around the country from champion to champion. It now runs every year, and I am very happy that it connects people and made my childhood dream come true.
