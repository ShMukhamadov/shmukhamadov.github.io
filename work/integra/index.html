What made it different

AI-driven personalized growth plans, backed by verified blockchain badges.

INTEGRA is an AI platform I founded to help students grow in three areas a report card doesn’t measure: skills, service, and physical health. It is built on Komil Inson, the idea of the complete person, and we have been testing it at my school since October 2024.

Since October 2024

Integrating AI into education

Founded and built an AI platform that gives students personalized growth plans in three core pillars – skills, service, and physical. Revolutionizing education with AI, integrating AI into education. Issues verified blockchain badges in order to make the platform more trustworthy. Designed for all teachers, students, school administration, and parents. Students upload reflections on their tasks that AI asked them to do, and AI tracks the progress, shows strengths and improvement areas by building a digital portfolio, making students’ college applications much easier. Testing it at the Presidential School in Jizzakh. Pitched to many venture funds and investors, receiving more than 60,000 USD in total.

  1. 01AssessA 40-question assessment maps each student’s knowledge, skills, languages, character strengths, and personality, so the first plan fits them.
  2. 02Plan and actEvery week the AI gives one skill task, one service task, and one physical task. Students complete them and upload evidence.
  3. 03ReflectStudents write a short reflection on each task. The AI reads it, scores it, and gives feedback.
  4. 04Earn and verifyTasks earn tokens toward Bronze, Silver, and Gold levels (50% skills, 30% service, 20% physical). Milestones become blockchain certificates the student owns.
