Since November 2024 · Founder & Secretary-General

Bringing Model UN to Jizzakh

Founded the first-ever Model United Nations in the Jizzakh region. I was accepted into the UNESCO Center for Peace’s fully funded program in the US but couldn’t participate due to some reasons and saw the highlights from the program, so I really wanted to do the same thing in Uzbekistan. I initiated JMUN and organized 3 MUN conferences in my region. Introduced 3,000+ students to UN-style diplomacy across these three conferences and 10+ events.

9–11 July 2025 · Izmir, Türkiye

ESDALMUN’25: Head Delegate

Led Uzbekistan’s delegation consisting of 7 members at the ESDALMUN’25 in Izmir, Türkiye, as Head Delegate. All delegates were part of JMUN, where I negotiated amendments, helped pass the final resolution, and coordinated logistical and all other required documents for our delegation’s participation.
