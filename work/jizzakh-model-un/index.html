The story

I was accepted to a fully funded program of the UNESCO Center for Peace in the United States, but in the end I couldn’t go. I watched its highlights online and decided students in Jizzakh should get that experience without leaving home. In November 2024, I founded Jizzakh Model United Nations, the first Model UN in our region. At ESDALMUN’25 in İzmir, I led our delegation, and I used the trip for a bigger goal: I agreed with ESDALMUN’s Secretary-General to build a much larger MUN together. A year later, that became Youth for Peace MUN.

Since November 2024 · Founder & Secretary-General

Bringing Model UN to Jizzakh

I founded the first Model United Nations in the Jizzakh region. I was accepted into the UNESCO Center for Peace’s fully funded program in the US but could not attend. Watching its highlights, I wanted to create the same experience in Uzbekistan. I initiated JMUN and organized 3 MUN conferences in my region. Across these three conferences and 10+ events, I introduced 3,000+ students to UN-style diplomacy.

9–11 July 2025 · İzmir, Türkiye

ESDALMUN’25: Head Delegate

As Head Delegate, I led a 7-member JMUN delegation from Uzbekistan at ESDALMUN’25 in İzmir, Türkiye. In committee, I negotiated amendments, helped pass the final resolution, and coordinated the logistics and all the documents our delegation needed to take part.
