About

Hi, I’m Shokhruz.

I’m from Jizzakh, Uzbekistan, and I come from a family of cotton and wheat farmers, so I learned early that the weather decides whether a year is good or bad. That is where my interest in climate comes from. Today I’m a Grade 11 student at the Presidential School in Jizzakh, and most of my time outside class goes into building things for young people.

I like to set big goals and then find the people to reach them with. When I founded Jizzakh Model United Nations, I wanted it to grow into a large international MUN in Central Asia, and less than two years later, together with the Youth Affairs Agency and almost every MUN team in Uzbekistan, we hosted 300 fully funded delegates at Youth for Peace MUN. When I joined the Young Climate Champions as a participant, I came back the next year as an organizer, mentor, and social media manager.

I’m most curious about economics, international relations, and how technology like AI can make education more personal. My first research project, at New Uzbekistan University’s Summer School in Economics, asked how urbanisation affects child mortality in Uzbekistan. Away from all of that, you’ll find me playing football, at a chessboard, in the pool, or traveling.
