Research

Questions I’m trying to answer with data

Economics · advisor: Prof. Wesley Sine, Cornell University · in progress

What protects small businesses when the dollar jumps?

Since I was a kid, sellers have given me the same answer whenever prices went up: the dollar went up. Now I’m testing that answer with data from six public sources and interviews with 8 business owners in Jizzakh. So far, a falling som hurts while a rising som doesn’t, and the damage shows up in sales and jobs while firms’ finances hold. Owners set prices in dollars and pass every change on to customers.

Pilot study of NextGen Diplomats · 2026

Did my diplomacy program work?

Fourteen students took the same test before and after five workshops, and four teachers marked 152 written answers blind. Knowledge rose from 68% to 89%, and confidence from 4.05 to 4.65 out of 5. Written reasoning grew less, and blind marking showed that our first marking had made that gain look 47% bigger.

Group paper · Summer School in Economics, New Uzbekistan University · July 2024

How does urbanization affect child mortality in Uzbekistan?

With seven teammates, I used World Bank data for 2010–2023 and a multiple regression. Years with more electricity access, more internet use and more safely managed sanitation had lower infant mortality. With only 14 yearly data points, this shows a pattern. It can’t show a cause.

Beyond the work

Hi, I’m Shokhruz.

I’m from Jizzakh, Uzbekistan, and my family farms cotton and wheat. When I’m home from boarding school, I help my father on the farm. That’s where climate change stopped being a headline for me. Farmers in our region now meet about water shortages, and my father has been convincing others to switch to drip irrigation, even though it costs more.

I’m most curious about economics, international relations, and how AI can make learning more personal. Away from all of that, you’ll find me playing football, at a chessboard, in the pool, or traveling.
